A place for your windows.
A shortcut to each one.

Open Contexts connects window switching with the way you organize a working day.

A persistent app bar

Keep window titles on the edge of your screen. Click to switch, or let the bar appear when your pointer reaches the edge.

Groups that remember

Make a group for code, research, or chat. Drag windows into place, and keep your custom groups after a restart.

A compact window list

⌘Tab shows individual windows by app, icon, and title in most-recently-used order. ⌘` narrows the list to the current app.

When another option
fits better.

Open Contexts currently has no window search, live thumbnails or trackpad gestures. For previews, compare AltTab, Sidebar and DockDoor. For search, compare Contexts, AltTab Pro, Sidebar and DockDoor. Consider uBar for a full taskbar, or DockDoor Pro for replacement-Dock profiles and widgets.

If you mostly switch between apps, the built-in macOS shortcuts may already cover your needs. For Open Contexts, you need macOS 13 or later and Accessibility permission to discover and focus windows.
