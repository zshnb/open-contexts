Decide what should happen to your Dock

If you want to retain the macOS Dock and add window previews, DockDoor Free is the relevant comparison. If you want a different Dock with profiles, widgets and per-display controls, evaluate DockDoor Pro.

Open Contexts adds a window list and an app bar positioned on the side or bottom. It can coexist with the native Dock, which remains the place to launch apps. Its saved groups are the reason to consider it here, rather than a complete replacement-Dock feature set.

Compare what each tool saves

Pro profiles hold Dock items such as apps, folders and widgets; AppSense can activate a profile based on the active application. Open Contexts groups hold selected window members and their order.

For example, a Dock profile can make your development apps available, while a window group can keep a specific browser window beside a specific editor and terminal. Neither description should be read as a guarantee that closed documents or an entire desktop layout will be restored.

Choose by the feature you would use every day

  • Choose DockDoor Free to add previews, Glance and searchable switching to your native-Dock workflow.
  • Evaluate DockDoor Pro for a replacement Dock with profiles, widgets and a Dock on each display.
  • Try Open Contexts for a title-list workflow and saved cross-app window groups.

Open Contexts searches app names and titles, but has no thumbnail previews. Both DockDoor apps request Screen Recording as well as Accessibility. Check permissions and your target macOS version before committing to a tool.
